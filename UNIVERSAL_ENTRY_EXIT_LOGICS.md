# 📘 Universal Confluence Engine: Entry & Exit Logics Reference

This document provides a comprehensive, tabular reference of all **Universal Entry Models**, **Universal Exit Models**, and **Macro Regime Filters** implemented in the 3-chart Confluence Engine across [backend/waterfall-backtest.js](file:///Users/viveksubbaiyah/TV-Strategy/backend/waterfall-backtest.js) and the dashboard [waterfall-backtest.html](file:///Users/viveksubbaiyah/TV-Strategy/waterfall-backtest.html).

All logics are **strictly universal** across all traded dates (**Oct 07, Oct 08, Oct 09, Oct 12, Oct 13, Oct 14, Oct 15, Oct 16, 2026**) with zero date-specific hardcoding.

---

## 🧭 Multi-Chart Architecture Overview

The confluence engine coordinates real-time 3-minute bars across three charts:
1. **Nifty 50 Spot Index** (`NSE_NIFTY, 3.csv` / `NSE_NIFTY, 3 (3).csv`) — Trend driver, CPR PP/S1/R1 pivot floor/ceiling, 200 EMA macro regime.
2. **Current Strike CE Option** (`NSE_...CE, 3.csv`) — Call side price action, CPR PP, 9 EMA, Sell Zone / Buy Zone, RMI Center.
3. **Current Strike PE Option** (`NSE_...PE, 3.csv`) — Put side price action, CPR PP, 9 EMA, Sell Zone / Buy Zone, RMI Center.
